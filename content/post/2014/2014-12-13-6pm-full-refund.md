接上篇，在6PM买到了一双尺码不同的奇葩鞋子，不知所措，去论坛发帖求助，很多人表示233，也有说可以免单的，建议联系客服。

值得庆幸的是这时我有一个英文不错的朋友，于是让他帮我写了封邮件。

> Dear Sir or Madam,
> 
> I bought a pair of leather shoes from your shop at Nov 8, 2014, and the order number is 199**472. However, when I opened the package, I found the two shoes are not the same size, which is showed in the picture below. I know this kind of shoes are out of stock, so I want to return the shoes and get my money back. I would appreciate it if you tell me the detailed return process.  
> yours sincerely  
> CM Tudou 

客服让我把鞋子退回。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3344" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-1.png" alt="6pm-full-refund-1" width="805" height="587" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-1.png 805w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-1-150x109.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-1-300x218.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 805px) 100vw, 805px" />][2]

邮件内容大概是说我现在人在中国，鞋子是朋友托回来的，国际运费很高，而且我不知道怎么发货。

> Dear lian,
> 
> I am glad to hear from you. I live in a small town in China, where no UPS can be found. Other expressages will be much more expensive as it is a international shipping. My oversea friend brought this shoes to me, and now he left. So I dont know how to ship this shoes to you. 

最后真的免单了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3346" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-2.png" alt="6pm-full-refund-2" width="1337" height="756" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-2.png 1337w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-2-150x84.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-2-300x169.png 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/12/6pm-full-refund-2-1024x579.png 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1337px) 100vw, 1337px" />][3]

钱很快就退到了我的虚拟信用卡，竟然还被扣了3块手续费，加上汇率的变动、转运费，这次海淘一共损失120多块，得到一只7.5码和一只10码的皮鞋&#8230;
