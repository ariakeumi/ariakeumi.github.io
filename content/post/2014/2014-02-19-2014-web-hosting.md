搬家迫在眉睫，数据备份好后，发现手头分文不剩。

赶紧问<a href="http://www.colg.cn/home.php?mod=space&uid=362702" target="_blank">某人</a>借钱，穷你妹啊！

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1564" alt="Zihao-Borrow-money" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/Zihao-Borrow-money.png" width="522" height="227" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/Zihao-Borrow-money.png 522w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/Zihao-Borrow-money-150x65.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/Zihao-Borrow-money-300x130.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 522px) 100vw, 522px" />][2]

不过一看规定傻眼了，4条命中3条，倒地不起。\_(:3ゝ∠)\_

进老薛主机网站首页一看，哎哟！美国主机50一年 ~ 碉堡了。

看到流量和空间就痿了，哪里够用啊！

不过好在提供附加包，加个1GB空间和10GB流量的包包就够用了。⊂((・▽・))⊃

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1568" alt="laoxuehost-Additional-services" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-Additional-services.png" width="934" height="606" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-Additional-services.png 934w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-Additional-services-150x97.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-Additional-services-300x194.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 934px) 100vw, 934px" />][5]

买了一年主机，两个附加包一个月，因为钱不够嘛。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1569" alt="laoxuehost-email" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-email.png" width="837" height="599" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-email.png 837w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-email-150x107.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-email-300x214.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 837px) 100vw, 837px" />][6]

老薛主机提供cPanel中文控制面板，很方便，以前一直没用过。

用<a href="http://wordpress.org/support/plugin/backwpup" target="_blank">BackWPup</a>把文件直接备份到新主机的FTP上，然后在cPanel里面直接解压，超级方便！

不过中间因为出了些问题，删除文件重新解压就好了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1570" alt="laoxue-banjia-trouble" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxue-banjia-trouble.png" width="1042" height="633" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxue-banjia-trouble.png 1042w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxue-banjia-trouble-150x91.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxue-banjia-trouble-300x182.png 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxue-banjia-trouble-1024x622.png 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1042px) 100vw, 1042px" />][7]

cPanel上的统计，如果用的流量少下个月就不用买流量附加包了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1571" alt="laoxuehost-cPanel" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-cPanel.png" width="243" height="433" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-cPanel.png 243w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-cPanel-84x150.png 84w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/laoxuehost-cPanel-168x300.png 168w" sizes="(max-width: 243px) 100vw, 243px" />][8]
