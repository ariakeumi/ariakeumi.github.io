突然在G+上看到有人谈到这个功能，果断去设置。

进入<a title="点击进入" href="https://www.google.com/settings/datatools" target="_blank">Google数据工具 &#8211; 账户设置</a>，就能看到限制账户管理员的设置了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1508" alt="google-inactive-1" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-1.png" width="466" height="193" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-1.png 466w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-1-150x62.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-1-300x124.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 466px) 100vw, 466px" />][1]

点进去，介绍，当你停止使用Google帐号时把Google账户的数据通过邮件发送给继承人。

很有意思的功能，就像遗嘱一样。天有不测风云，设置了有备无患。

腾讯可以效仿，XX个月没登录QQ了自动把Q币发送给信任联系人什么的。☆\*:.｡. o(≧▽≦)o .｡.:\*☆

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-1509" alt="google-inactive-2" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-2.png" width="740" height="671" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-2.png 757w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-2-150x136.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-2-300x272.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 740px) 100vw, 740px" />][2]

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-large wp-image-1510" alt="google-inactive-3" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-3.png" width="548" height="374" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-3.png 548w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-3-150x102.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/02/google-inactive-3-300x204.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 548px) 100vw, 548px" />][3]
