Windows上大名鼎鼎的垃圾清理软件终于推出了Android版，该来的总是会来的。

现在暂时还是Beta版（公测），加入CCleaner For Android的G+社群就可以参加测试了。

[blockquote source=&#8221;小众软件&#8221;]

  1. 加入 [CCleaner for Android 社群][1]
  2. 前往 [Google Play][2]
  3. 点击 “成为测试人员”
  4. 最后才可以从 Google Play 下载。</blockquote> 

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1938" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-1.png" alt="ccleaner-for-android-beta-1" width="432" height="720" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-1.png 768w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-1-90x150.png 90w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-1-180x300.png 180w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-1-614x1024.png 614w" sizes="(max-width: 432px) 100vw, 432px" />][3]

Google Play上的CC，暂时还不能评论。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1939" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-2.png" alt="ccleaner-for-android-beta-2" width="432" height="720" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-2.png 768w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-2-90x150.png 90w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-2-180x300.png 180w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-2-614x1024.png 614w" sizes="(max-width: 432px) 100vw, 432px" />][4]

界面非常简洁，符合<a title="Android Design" href="http://www.apkbus.com/design/" target="_blank">Android Design</a>。

都是英文压力很大，点“ANALYZE”应该是分析垃圾吧，操纵逻辑和Windows版的一样。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1940" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-31.png" alt="ccleaner-for-android-beta-3" width="432" height="720" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-31.png 768w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-31-90x150.png 90w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-31-180x300.png 180w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-31-614x1024.png 614w" sizes="(max-width: 432px) 100vw, 432px" />][5]

分析完了，就缓存、chipboard（什么玩意）和通话记录，清理缓存的时候好像把快图浏览的略缩图干掉了。\_(:3ゝ∠)\_

<!--more-->

清理出668MB缓存，好像效果还不错。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1942" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-4.png" alt="ccleaner-for-android-beta-4" width="432" height="720" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-4.png 768w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-4-90x150.png 90w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-4-180x300.png 180w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-4-614x1024.png 614w" sizes="(max-width: 432px) 100vw, 432px" />][6]

程序管理自然也少不了，也可以卸载系统程序（肯定需要ROOT），不过能卸载的系统程序只有我用GAPPS刷上去的Google系应用和CM自带的终端模拟器，CC在这方面很保守。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1947" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-52.png" alt="ccleaner-for-android-beta-5" width="432" height="720" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-52.png 768w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-52-90x150.png 90w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-52-180x300.png 180w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-52-614x1024.png 614w" sizes="(max-width: 432px) 100vw, 432px" />][7]

还可以查看系统信息，好像很鸡肋。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-1945" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-61.png" alt="ccleaner-for-android-beta-6" width="432" height="720" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-61.png 768w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-61-90x150.png 90w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-61-180x300.png 180w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2014/04/ccleaner-for-android-beta-61-614x1024.png 614w" sizes="(max-width: 432px) 100vw, 432px" />][8]
