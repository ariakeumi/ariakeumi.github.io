大概半年前，我的 Z3C 摔了几次，然后后盖就碎成这样了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="1094" height="730" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/05/Z3C-WW.jpg" alt="" class="wp-image-3892" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/05/Z3C-WW.jpg 1094w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/05/Z3C-WW-150x100.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/05/Z3C-WW-300x200.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/05/Z3C-WW-1024x683.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1094px) 100vw, 1094px" />][1] 

本来还碎的挺好看的，不过后来一些玻璃渣开始掉落，加上裂缝中进了很多灰尘，实在没办法，只能换后盖了，然后发现淘宝竟然有十几块包邮的Z3C后盖，毫不犹豫拍下。

直接暴力手段卸开后来，iPad 拍摄渣画质。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="778" height="581" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-1.jpg" alt="sony-z3c-cover-1" class="wp-image-4782" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-1.jpg 778w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-1-150x112.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-1-300x224.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 778px) 100vw, 778px" />][2] [<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" width="778" height="581" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-2.jpg" alt="sony-z3c-cover-2" class="wp-image-4783" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-2.jpg 778w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-2-150x112.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2016/02/sony-z3c-cover-2-300x224.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 778px) 100vw, 778px" />][3]
