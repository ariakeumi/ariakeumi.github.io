昨天不愉快的事还没结束，退款还得等，手头只剩2K多，只能买Z3C了，果断上了。

第二位卖家准时发货，今天准时收到。

盒子超小，一个巴掌大。[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3553" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-1.jpg" alt="sony-xperia-z3c-1" width="1680" height="1260" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-1.jpg 1680w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-1-150x113.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-1-300x225.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-1-1024x768.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1680px) 100vw, 1680px" />][1]

本体，绿色的边框太骚了！[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3554" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-2.jpg" alt="sony-xperia-z3c-2" width="1680" height="1260" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-2.jpg 1680w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-2-150x113.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-2-300x225.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-2-1024x768.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1680px) 100vw, 1680px" />][2]

日版Z3C的后面印着运营商docomo的LOGO而不是索尼的，信仰分-1。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3555" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-3.jpg" alt="sony-xperia-z3c-3" width="1680" height="1260" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-3.jpg 1680w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-3-150x113.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-3-300x225.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-3-1024x768.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1680px) 100vw, 1680px" />][3]

低端卡片相机（DSC-W730）在弱光环境不开闪光灯图片没法看，开了又拍不好。[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3556" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-4.jpg" alt="sony-xperia-z3c-4" width="1680" height="1260" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-4.jpg 1680w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-4-150x113.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-4-300x225.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-4-1024x768.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1680px) 100vw, 1680px" />][4]

标配没有数据线和电源适配器，只有一个TV信号接收转换头和磁吸式座充及扣具。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3557" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-5.jpg" alt="sony-xperia-z3c-5" width="1680" height="1260" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-5.jpg 1680w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-5-150x113.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-5-300x225.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-5-1024x768.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1680px) 100vw, 1680px" />][5]

使用FlashTool强刷了港版的Z3C固件（D5833\_23.0.A.2.93\_HK），第一次刷的时候失败了，以为变砖了，再来一遍就好了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3558" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-6.jpg" alt="sony-xperia-z3c-6" width="1680" height="1255" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-6.jpg 1680w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-6-150x112.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-6-300x224.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/sony-xperia-z3c-6-1024x765.jpg 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 1680px) 100vw, 1680px" />][6]

刷了旧的港版固件后，使用 giefroot_v3 进行ROOT和刷入REC，傻瓜式的。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3559" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/xperia-z3c-7.png" alt="xperia-z3c-7" width="877" height="523" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/xperia-z3c-7.png 877w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/xperia-z3c-7-150x89.png 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/03/xperia-z3c-7-300x179.png 300w" sizes="(max-width: 877px) 100vw, 877px" />][7]
