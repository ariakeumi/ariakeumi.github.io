10051升级10061。

桌面，回收站图标又换了，还是好丑&#8230;

任务栏变成默认黑色半透明（相对Build 10041等），在设置项的个性化里面可以设置随壁纸色调变化。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3741" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-desktop.jpg" alt="windows-10-build-10061-desktop" width="1280" height="800" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-desktop.jpg 1280w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-desktop-150x94.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-desktop-300x188.jpg 300w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-desktop-1024x640.jpg 1024w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-desktop-400x250.jpg 400w" sizes="(max-width: 1280px) 100vw, 1280px" />][1]

开始菜单小改，把电源选项隐藏到 “功率” 内，这里好像翻译错了，应该是电源（Power）。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3742" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-startmenu.jpg" alt="windows-10-build-10061-startmenu" width="777" height="637" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-startmenu.jpg 777w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-startmenu-150x123.jpg 150w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-startmenu-300x246.jpg 300w" sizes="(max-width: 777px) 100vw, 777px" />][2]

日历好看了很多，要是再能看到节假日就好了。

[<img loading="lazy" decoding="async" class="alignnone size-full wp-image-3746" src="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-Calendar.png" alt="windows-10-build-10061-Calendar" width="486" height="599" srcset="https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-Calendar.png 486w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-Calendar-122x150.png 122w, https://wp-archive.baka.li/2015/04/windows-10-build-10061-Calendar-243x300.png 243w" sizes="(max-width: 486px) 100vw, 486px" />][3]

应用程序窗口边框不能随壁纸变换色调了，统一变成浅灰色，非焦点窗口为深灰色，好丑！
